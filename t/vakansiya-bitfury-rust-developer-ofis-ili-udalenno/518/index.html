The Bitfury Group develops and delivers both the software and the hardware solutions necessary for businesses, governments, organizations and individuals to securely move an asset across the Blockchain. The expertise of The Bitfury Group ensures successful, easy, fast, secure and cost-effective connectivity to the Blockchain.

We are looking for a Rust expert to join our team to design functionality and solutions for one of our cutting-edge products.

Location: one of our offices OR remote.

Duties and responsibilities

  • Design, code, test, debug, and deliver features and improvements for a number of applications (including multiplatform ones) in a continuous manner
  • Refactor code as necessary
  • Perform code reviews for best practices
  • Maintain high code coverage with tests
  • Collaborate with frontend developers, business analysts, QA & DevOps engineers etc as required for project needs
  • Mentor less experienced team members

Qualifications

Must:

  • 3+ years commercial programming experience (including 0.5+ years programming in Rust)
  • Strong knowledge of software engineering (algorithms/data structures/design principles)
  • Scripting languages experience, OOP-concepts understanding, understanding of interfaces, test driven development
  • gRPC and REST APIs
  • Linux user
  • Maven, Git
  • Motivated and fast learning
  • Pro-active team worker
  • At least intermediate English.

Nice to have:

  • Docker, docker-compose
  • Experience with distributed systems, like cryptocurrencies and blockchain technologies
  • Knowledge in multi-threading programming, network programming, cryptography
